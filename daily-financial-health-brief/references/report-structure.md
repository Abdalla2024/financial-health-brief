# Report structure

`deliverables/report.md` is concise and puts the owner's decisions first, as the manager asked.

1. **Decisions needed: unresolved queue.** Every pending or disputed row from the month start through the reporting date, with amount (or unknown) and the action. Disputed refunds are called out.
2. **Key figures.** Five exact, signed, labelled numbers: reporting-date posted, pending-confirmed and disputed-confirmed totals; prior business day posted total; and the change between the two posted totals (reporting minus prior). Notes on unknown amounts excluded.
3. **Budget variance, month to date.** One row per budgeted category with budget, month-to-date posted, variance, the 10% figure and the result (MATERIAL OVER, MATERIAL UNDER or within tolerance).
4. **Revenue and outstanding balances.** Collected revenue and outstanding balance for both dates with the change, or an UNRESOLVED line where a snapshot is missing.
5. **Exceptions and clarification requests.** Each addressed to Operations or Finance.
6. **Traceability.** The ledger rows behind each key figure and each category's variance, and the revenue rows behind each revenue figure.
7. **Source metadata.** URL, spreadsheet ID, tab and gid, fetch time (UTC), source version, rows fetched and checksum for each source.

The brief is headed as a draft for human review. Every table is followed by a source line giving source identity, version, date or period and currency.
